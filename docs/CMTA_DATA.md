## Service Timings

- **Operating Hours:** 6:00 AM – 10:00 PM (daily)
- **Peak hours frequency:** Every 5–10 minutes
- **Off-peak frequency:** Every 10–15 minutes
- **Electric Bus (PIMS–Bari Imam):** Every 10 minutes

## Data Gaps / Limitations

- Exact station-by-station fares not published
- Blue Line and Green Line do not have a standardized ticketing system yet (as of late 2024)
- Real-time bus tracking not available via public API
- Senior citizen discount is proposed/mentioned in project but not officially confirmed by CMTA
